C1

Shade

Under the pavilion. Mingling, tables, food. Your default.

  • ExposureS · 1/500 locked
  • ISOAUTO180–12800
  • FocusC · Tracking · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker ALL
  • DriveCL
  • ExpectISO 400–1600

Stepping outside? → C2. Today is gray more than it's bright.

Why
  • Under a roof there isn't light for both shutter and depth. Lock the shutter; the camera opens to f/4 and ISO pays the difference.
  • S is a hard lock. In A mode the minimum-shutter setting is only a target — it quietly drops below when ISO caps out.
  • Flicker ALL stays on in case the pavilion has fixtures running.
  • Posed group under the roof? Use C4 instead — f/4 won't hold two rows.

C2

Cloud

Outside under clouds. Your outdoor default today.

  • ExposureA · f/5.6
  • ISOAUTO2floor 1/1000
  • FocusC · Tracking · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker OFF

Back under the roof? → C1. One click.

Why
  • Sky cover runs 50–76% all day, so this is where you live outdoors — not C3.
  • There's enough light that 1/1000 comes free from the AUTO2 floor. Shutter isn't the scarce thing out here; depth is.
  • That's why A, not S. S at 1/500 under overcast puts you at f/4 wide open with no margin.

C3

Sun

Outside when the sun breaks through.

  • ExposureA · f/8
  • ISOAUTO2floor 1/1000
  • FocusC · Tracking · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker OFF
  • DriveCL
  • Expect1/1000 @ ISO 200

C2 ↔ C3 is one click. The sun is in and out all day — don't overthink which one you're on.

Why
  • One stop tighter than C2 because the light is there to spend on depth.
  • 11:30–2:00 is the worst window for anyone standing in the open — sun peaks at 51° and you get raccoon eyes. Move posed shots to the pavilion's open edge.
  • Sunlight doesn't flicker; reduction off keeps the full burst rate.

C4

Group

Posed groups. Departments, teams, the whole company.

  • ExposureA · f/8
  • ISOAUTO1floor 1/250
  • FocusSwitch S · Zone · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker ALL
  • DriveCL — burst for blinks
  • ExpectISO ~1000 shade~200 in sun

Flip the focus switch to S. And flip it back to C when you're done — it's physical, the slot won't do it for you.

Why
  • f/8 on this sensor is about f/6.3 of full-frame depth — enough for three rows at normal distance.
  • Don't reach for f/11. You're at the diffraction threshold for 102MP and you'd trade the back row's sharpness for everyone's.
  • The 1/250 floor is what lets one slot work from pavilion shade to full sun without touching anything.
  • Zone with face detect on will grab someone's face; f/8 covers the rest. Zone position resets — re-place it.

C5

Action

Handshakes, applause, people walking. Kids on rides.

  • ExposureS · 1/500 locked
  • ISOAUTO180–12800
  • FocusC · Zone · SET 2 · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker ALL
  • DriveCH
  • ExpectISO 400–1600

Rides: spin the shutter dial to 1/2000. It reverts when you leave the slot — nothing to re-save. 1/500 will not freeze a car on the Racer.

Why
  • The shutter dial is live in S mode, so this slot stretches from a handshake to a coaster without a rebuild.
  • Zone covers where the action happens. SET 2 is meant to hold your subject when someone crosses in front.
  • CH gives the most frames at the peak moment. Zone position resets — re-place it when you land here.

  • Single frames for deliberate, timed shots — you don't need thirty of one sentence.

Remember

  • The pavilion edge is the hard part. Subject in shade against sunlit background is 3–5 stops. Expose for the face and let the background go, or reframe so it's shaded too.
  • Focus switch is physical. C everywhere, S on C4, back to C after.
  • Shutter won't fire? AF-C priority is FOCUS — the camera refuses until it confirms. Grab a higher-contrast edge or recompose. It also throttles the CH burst on C5. Nothing is broken.
  • IS greyed out? It's the O.I.S. switch on the lens barrel, not the menu.
  • Zone position resets — re-place it on C4 and C5.
  • No slot uses electronic shutter. The old Silent slot is gone; C4 is Group now.
  • Changes at the shoot won't stick. Auto update is DISABLE, so the shutter dial trick on C5 is safe — it reverts on its own.
  • Let the kit acclimate before you pull it out. 78°F and humid against an air-conditioned car fogs the front element.
