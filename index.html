Why

  • f/4 is the most light the 45-100 gives you.
  • Auto ISO holds 1/250 so talking hands stay sharp. ISO rises instead.
  • Tracking + face detect follows whoever moves through the frame.
  • Short CL bursts catch the best expression without flooding the cards.

Watch for

  • Groups: turn to f/5.6 by hand and flip the focus switch to S. Burst a few frames to beat blinks.
  • In a very dim room, once ISO hits 12800 the shutter may slip below 1/250.

Why

  • S mode locks 1/500 to freeze clapping hands and walking.
  • Zone covers the area where the action happens.
  • SET 2 is meant to stay on your subject when someone crosses in front.
  • CH gives you the most frames at the peak moment.

Watch for

  • Dim room ≈ ISO 10,000, close to the cap. If frames go dark, drop to 1/250.
  • If ALL FRAMES slows the burst too much, switch to FIRST FRAME and re-save C2.

Why

  • The speaker's lighting stays constant. M holds the shutter and aperture while Auto ISO adjusts.
  • 1/250 freezes gestures.
  • Single point with face detect OFF keeps focus from jumping to faces in the audience.
  • Single frames for deliberate, timed shots.

Why

  • The electronic shutter is silent.
  • Flicker reduction doesn't work with the electronic shutter, so the shutter speed has to prevent banding.
  • ~1/120 = one full flicker cycle per line of the frame, which should even out the bands (reasoning, not from the manual).
  • Same focus setup as C3, since it's the same subject.

On-site test

  1. Shoot a blank wall, zoom to 100%, look for bands.
  2. Bands → fine-tune with Flickerless.
  3. Still banding → try 1/60.
  4. No bands at all (steady lights) → try 1/250.
  5. Shoot a hand mid-gesture and check for skew.
  6. Found a good value → re-save C4.
  7. Fails → use C3 during talks.

Why

  • Lots of light, so f/8 gives extra depth at no cost.
  • A 1/2000 minimum keeps everything crisp. Expect about ISO 400.
  • Sunlight doesn't flicker. Turning reduction off keeps the full burst speed.

Watch for

  • Walking back inside? Switch to C1. This slot has no flicker protection.

Remember

  • The focus switch is physical, so it's probably not saved with a slot. Keep it on C; switch to S for posed groups.
  • Changes made at the shoot won't stick. Auto Update is off. To keep a change: set it on P/S/A/M, then IMAGE QUALITY SETTING › EDIT/SAVE CUSTOM SETTING › Cx › SAVE CURRENT SETTINGS.
  • Banding or brightness jumping between frames = flicker. C1–C3 are protected. C4 relies on its shutter speed. C5 and C6 have no protection.
  • Movie slots were not changed. Keep the STILL/MOVIE switch on STILL.
