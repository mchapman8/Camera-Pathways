Ref
C1

Candid

People mingling, talking, reacting. Your default indoors.

  • ExposureA · f/4 · ≥1/250
  • FocusSwitch C · Tracking · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker ALL
  • DriveCL

Posed group? Turn to f/5.6 and flip the focus switch to S. Burst a few frames for blinks.

Why
  • f/4 is the most light the lens gives you.
  • Auto ISO (AUTO1) holds 1/250 so hands stay sharp. ISO rises instead, up to 12800.
  • Tracking + face detect follows whoever moves through the frame.
  • Short CL bursts catch the best expression.
  • Very dim room: once ISO hits 12800, the shutter may slip below 1/250.

C2

Move

Handshakes, applause, people walking. Split-second moments.

  • ExposureS · 1/500
  • FocusSwitch C · Zone · SET 2 · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker ALL
  • DriveCH

Frames coming out dark? Dim room — drop to 1/250.

Why
  • S locks 1/500 to freeze clapping hands and walking.
  • Auto ISO (AUTO3) only uses its 12800 cap here. A dim room needs about ISO 10,000.
  • Zone covers the area where the action happens. SET 2 is meant to stay on your subject when someone crosses in front.
  • CH gives the most frames at the peak moment.
  • If ALL FRAMES slows the burst too much, switch to FIRST FRAME and re-save C2.

C3

Speaker

Someone presenting. Shutter sound is OK.

  • ExposureM · f/4 · 1/250
  • FocusSwitch C · Single point · Face OFF
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker ALL
  • DriveS (single)

Bright screen behind them? The meter can be fooled — check the histogram.

Why
  • The speaker's light stays steady. M holds shutter and aperture while Auto ISO (AUTO3, cap 12800) adjusts.
  • 1/250 freezes gestures.
  • Single point with face detect OFF keeps focus from jumping to faces in the audience.
  • Single frames for deliberate, timed shots.

C4

Silent

Quiet moment during a talk. When shutter noise would be noticed.

  • ExposureM · f/4 · ~1/120
  • FocusSwitch C · Single point · Face OFF
  • ShutterElectronic · Flickerless ON
  • DriveS (single)

Test before relying on it. If it fails, use C3.

On-site test
  1. Shoot a blank wall, zoom to 100%, look for bands.
  2. Bands → fine-tune with Flickerless (saved at 1/125).
  3. Still banding → try 1/60.
  4. No bands at all → try 1/250.
  5. Shoot a hand mid-gesture and check for skew.
  6. Found a good value → re-save C4.
Why
  • The electronic shutter is silent.
  • Flicker reduction doesn't work with it, so the shutter speed has to prevent banding.
  • ~1/120 = one full flicker cycle per line of the frame (reasoning, not from the manual).
  • Same focus setup as C3, since it's the same subject.

C5

Sun

Outside in bright sun.

  • ExposureA · f/8 · ≥1/2000
  • FocusSwitch C · Tracking · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker OFF
  • DriveCL

Back inside? Switch to C1 — this slot has no flicker protection.

Why
  • Lots of light, so f/8 gives extra depth at no cost.
  • Auto ISO (AUTO3) holds 1/2000. Expect about ISO 400.
  • Sunlight doesn't flicker. Reduction off keeps the full burst speed.

C6

Overcast

Outside under clouds or on a gray day.

  • ExposureA · f/5.6 · ≥1/1000
  • FocusSwitch C · Tracking · Face ON
  • ShutterMechanical · Flicker OFF
  • DriveCL

Back inside? Switch to C1 — this slot has no flicker protection.

Why
  • Less light than sun, so one stop wider than C5. Expect about ISO 400–1,600.
  • Auto ISO (AUTO2) holds 1/1000, which still freezes people moving.
  • No flicker outside. Reduction off keeps the full burst speed.

Remember

  • Focus switch is physical — probably not saved with a slot. C for all slots, S for posed groups.
  • Changes at the shoot won't stick. To keep one, set it on P/S/A/M, then IMAGE QUALITY SETTING › EDIT/SAVE CUSTOM SETTING › Cx › SAVE CURRENT SETTINGS
  • Banding or brightness jumping = flicker. C1–C3 protected · C4 relies on its shutter speed · C5–C6 unprotected.
  • Movie slots unchanged. Keep the switch on STILL.
